The Oak Street kitchen had served the family for 30 years but was showing every year of its age. Dated laminate counters, closed-off layout, and failing appliances made it a source of frustration rather than a gathering place.

We started with a structural assessment — the wall separating the kitchen from the dining room was load-bearing, so we engineered a flush beam to carry the load and opened up nearly 400 square feet of combined living space.

The clients used our guided design process to land on their style profile: **Modern Warm** — clean lines and neutral palette, but with natural materials and aged metal accents to keep it from feeling cold. From that profile, every decision (cabinet door style, counter material, lighting) had a clear reference point.

The result is a kitchen that the whole family actually wants to spend time in.
